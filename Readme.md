# De Eclat - Project Overview & AI Context Guide

## 1. Introduction
This repository contains the promotional website for **De Eclat** (in partnership with DIALUSTER INC.). 
**AI ASSISTANT INSTRUCTION:** Read this document first to understand the company's story, market positioning, and brand vibe before writing any content or code. Refer to `design.md` for specific typography, color palettes, and component structures.

## 2. The Brand Story & History
* **Legacy:** De Eclat has been an established authority in the jewelry industry for over **15 years**.
* **Locations:** Headquartered primarily in the heart of **Hong Kong** (Jordan, Kowloon), with a strong secondary presence in **Tokyo, Japan** (Ueno). 
* **Core Offerings:** We specialize in the sourcing and trading of:
  * High-grade Diamonds
  * Precious Stones
  * Luxury Watches

## 3. Market Positioning & Target Audience
This is **not** a retail e-commerce website. It is a corporate promotion and service platform designed to attract and build trust with our core demographic.

* **Primary Audience:** **B2B (Business-to-Business).** We cater heavily to other businesses, jewelers, and high-volume buyers.
* **Competitive Edge:** * **Aggressive Pricing:** We provide unmatched value without compromising the product.
  * **Top-Tier Quality:** We strictly compete in the "high average" to "best quality" tiers of goods. We do not deal in low-end stones.
* **Tone of Voice:** Professional, established, transparent, and quietly confident. We do not need to use aggressive sales copy; the quality and pricing of our 15-year supply chain speak for themselves.

## 4. The "Vibe" & Aesthetic Direction
While the brand carries a historic, established weight (as seen in our business card typography), the digital aesthetic must evoke a **"Very Soft and Spring Weather"** feeling.

* **Visual Interpretation for AI:**
  * **Atmosphere:** Ethereal, fresh, bright, and inviting. Think of a luxury boutique on a crisp, clear spring morning.
  * **Lighting:** Photography and assets should feature soft, natural, diffused lighting—avoiding harsh shadows or overly dark, moody backdrops.
  * **UI Translation:** Use generous, airy whitespace (padding/margins). Backgrounds should lean into soft pearl whites or very pale warm tones. Borders and dividers should be delicate. 
  * **Contrast:** The deep ruby/crimson from our branding will serve as a grounding accent against this soft, spring-like canvas, drawing the eye precisely where it needs to go.

## 5. Website Objectives
1. **Showcase Authority:** Highlight 15+ years of expertise and top-tier inventory.
2. **Facilitate B2B Inquiries:** Direct potential partners and buyers to our highly controlled contact channels (email inquiry form, or manual phone dialing for HK and Japan).
3. **Brand Elevation:** Provide a digital "business card" that matches the physical prestige of the company.

## 6. Technical Stack Context
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Focus on utility classes for soft UI, e.g., `shadow-sm`, `bg-opacity-50`, `font-light`, `tracking-wide`).
* **Architecture:** Static/Promotional site (HTML/CSS/JS). No backend shopping cart or checkout flows.
