# De Eclat — Voice & tone

## Brand essence

**Quietly confident authority** in diamonds, stones, and timepieces. The voice reflects Old World luxury: measured, precise, never loud or promotional.

---

## Personality

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Disciplined, expert | Hype-driven or “deal” language |
| Invitation, not pressure | Urgent scarcity (“Buy now”) |
| International, rooted in HK | Casual slang or meme tone |
| B2B-trusted partner | Mass-market retail cheer |

---

## Writing principles

1. **Short sentences, generous space** — mirror visual whitespace.
2. **Specific over superlative** — “15+ years” not “world’s best.”
3. **Service-oriented** — private viewings, cutting, servicing, appointments.
4. **Geography with precision** — Hong Kong headquarters when site is HK-focused.

---

## Sample lines (on-brand)

- “A quietly confident authority in diamonds, stones and timepieces.”
- “Begin a measured conversation with De Eclat.”
- “For over 15 years, De Eclat has served jewelers, buyers and trusted partners…”

---

## Sample lines (off-brand)

- “Unbeatable prices on bling!”
- “DM us on WhatsApp for instant deals!”
- “The #1 diamond shop in Asia!!!”

---

## Channels

| Channel | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Website | Formal, warm, minimal |
| Email inquiry replies | Courteous, concise, sign with full brand name |
| Legal/footer | Neutral, factual |

---

## Sub-brands

Use **display serif, uppercase, tracked** for legal entities (e.g. DIALUSTER INC.) — distinct from the De Eclat script mark.
