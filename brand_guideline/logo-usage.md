## Clear space

Minimum clear space on all sides = **height of the icon mark** (or cap height of script “D”), whichever is larger in the lockup.

```
        ┌── clear ──┐
        │  [icon]   │
        │  De Eclat │
        └── clear ──┘
```

---

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Digital min | Print min |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Icon alone | 32×32 px | 8 mm |
| Script wordmark | 120 px width | 25 mm width |
| Horizontal lockup | 200 px width | 40 mm width |

Below minimums, use **icon only** (favicon, social avatar).

## Correct usage

- Keep proportions; scale uniformly.
- Use provided SVG files.
- On web, nav may use live text in Pinyon Script matching wordmark proportions.

---

## Incorrect usage

- Do not rotate, skew, or stretch.
- Do not change ruby to another red or add gradients to the mark.
- Do not place ruby wordmark on busy imagery without scrim.
- Do not recreate the icon in a different style.
- Do not separate “De” and “Eclat” onto two lines except in approved stacked lockups (future).
- Do not outline or drop-shadow the script wordmark for “legibility” on pearl (adjust background instead).

---

## Co-branding

Partner logos should be separated by at least **2× clear space**. De Eclat lockup should not be smaller than the partner mark.

Sub-brands on card (e.g. DIALUSTER INC., Hiba): set in **Playfair Display**, uppercase with wide tracking—not in Pinyon Script.

---

## Favicon & social

1. Export PNG from `deeclat-icon-primary.svg` at 32, 180, 512 px.
2. Use script wordmark only for wide banners (LinkedIn cover), not cropped icon.
