# De Eclat — Photography & imagery

## Direction

- **Product-first:** Jewelry, watches, and stones shot with clarity; minimal overlay text on photos.
- **Lighting:** Soft, high-end studio or natural light; avoid harsh flash or cluttered backgrounds.
- **Color grade:** Neutral-to-warm; align highlights with pearl/champagne, not cool blue casts.

---

## Hero & backgrounds

- Full-viewport hero may use illustration (`hero-diamond.svg`) or future video/still of diamond or timepiece.
- Always preserve legibility: pearl overlay or gradient when text sits on imagery.

---

---

## Icons & illustration

- Line weight and color should harmonize with ruby at low opacity or charcoal.
- Do not use cartoon or flat consumer-retail icon sets.

---

## Do not

- Stock photos that conflict with B2B luxury positioning.
- Heavy filters, lens flare, or watermarking over the brand mark.
- Busy patterns behind script logo without scrim.
